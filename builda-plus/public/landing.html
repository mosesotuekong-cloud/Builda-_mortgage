What we do

Inefficiency rarely sits where you expect it.

In a mortgage firm, the work that slows cases down is spread across roles and systems. Leadership sees the outcome (delays, rework, chasing) but not always the cause. We look for evidence of where time is lost across the whole operation.

  • Client onboarding

    Enquiry handling, initial disclosure and identity checks that depend on manual steps.

  • Fact finding

    Information gathered more than once, or in formats that need re-entering later.

  • Document collection

    Outstanding documents tracked through memory, spreadsheets or repeated emails.

  • Application processing

    Data keyed again between CRM, sourcing tools and lender portals.

  • Underwriting coordination

    Lender queries and conditions passed between advisers, case managers and clients.

  • Case progression

    Status updates to clients, introducers and solicitors handled one call at a time.

  • Completion

    Final checks and conveyancer coordination that rely on individual follow-up.

  • Renewals and aftercare

    Product expiries and reviews that depend on someone remembering to act.

Every firm is different. These are places we look, not problems we assume you have. builda+ only reports what the evidence from your firm supports.

How it works

From how work happens to what should change.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand how work actually happens.

    Independent, role-based interviews with advisers, case managers and administrators, alongside information your firm already holds. Each person is asked only about the work they do.

  2. 02

    Identify

    Find operational inefficiencies and opportunities.

    Accounts are compared across roles to surface bottlenecks, hidden admin and differing views. Each finding is linked to its evidence and given a confidence level.

  3. 03

    Transform

    Design and implement appropriate solutions.

    Every finding is classified before anything is built. The priorities you approve become implementation-ready plans, delivered with JADA.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Understand the impact of the changes.

    A baseline is recorded before anything changes, and success measures are agreed in advance, so results can be compared honestly.

Our approach

Not everything needs AI.

The most useful answer isn't always a new tool. Some problems come from how a process is designed. Some need a simple operational change. Some suit conventional automation, a few genuinely benefit from AI, and some should stay with people. builda+ classifies every finding before recommending anything.

  • Overhaul

    The process itself needs redesigning.

    Steps that exist for historical reasons, or work that loops back on itself. Automating it would only speed up the wrong thing.

  • Fix

    A simple operational change solves it.

    A clearer checklist, a single owner, a better handoff, or a feature you already pay for, set up properly.

  • Automate

    Repetitive, structured work suited to automation.

    Conventional automation first; AI only where it adds something. Staff approve client communications and record changes.

  • Keep human

    Judgement, accountability and relationships.

    Advice, suitability decisions and client conversations stay with qualified people.

Fix the work before automating it.

Trust and responsible AI

Responsible by design.

  • Evidence before recommendations

    Every finding shows where it came from (what your team said, what was measured, or what is still an assumption) with a confidence level. Missing figures stay missing; they're never invented.

  • Human accountability

    builda+ advises and prepares. Your people make the decisions, and every correction to a finding is recorded.

  • Privacy and data minimisation

    Participants' names are not shared with the AI. Findings are presented by role, not by person, and client information is kept out of discovery.

  • Appropriate use of AI

    AI interprets conversations and words questions. Calculations, scoring and business rules run in deterministic software.

  • Measurable outcomes

    Recommendations are tied to operational measures agreed in advance, so improvement is judged on evidence rather than expectation.
